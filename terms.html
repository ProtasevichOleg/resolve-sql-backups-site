Last updated: 12 October 2026

Purpose and access

Resolve SQL Backups is a personal automation tool for backing up its owner's DaVinci Resolve PostgreSQL project libraries. It is not a public backup service and does not offer public registration, paid subscriptions, or service guarantees.

Google account access requires explicit authorization by the account owner. The tool may only be used with accounts and data that the operator is authorized to access.

Operator responsibilities

The operator is responsible for protecting credentials and encryption passwords, choosing notification recipients, configuring retention, monitoring available storage, and periodically checking that backups can be restored.

Retention rules may delete older backup files. Deleting a backup or losing its encryption password may prevent recovery.

Availability

The tool is provided as is. Successful backup and notification delivery depend on the source database, network access, available storage, valid authorization, and Google services. A backup should not be considered a guarantee of recovery until its restoration has been tested.

Google services and privacy

Use of Google Drive and Gmail remains subject to Google's applicable terms and policies. The tool's handling of data is described in its Privacy Policy.

Stopping use

The owner may stop the automation and revoke its Google account access at any time. Previously uploaded files and sent emails remain until separately deleted.
